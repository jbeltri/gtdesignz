Common questions

Useful answers before you begin

What information do you need to start a project?

A property address, a short description of the work, any existing drawings or photographs, and your preferred timescale are enough for an initial review.

Can GT Designz help with planning and Building Regulations?

Yes. We prepare planning drawings and detailed Building Regulations packages, and coordinate responses with Building Control and specialist consultants.

Do you provide structural calculations?

Structural coordination is available for common alterations, including steel beams, padstones, openings and foundation solutions.

What types of property do you work on?

Our experience includes houses, extensions, outbuildings, shops, mixed-use buildings, heritage properties and industrial premises.

Can you update drawings prepared by another consultant?

Yes. Existing information can be reviewed, coordinated and developed into clear revised drawings for the next project stage.

Which areas do you cover?

We support projects across London, the West Midlands, Milton Keynes and other locations in England, subject to the brief.
