What each project needed, and how the team added clarity

These summaries are based on verified project correspondence. Residential locations are intentionally generalised while retaining the service, building type and technical knowledge relevant to future clients.

01

Retail · Guildford

Multi-level lease plan coordination

The brief A retail property required a single coordinated drawing showing basement and ground-floor areas, tenant demise, fire exits, lobbies and the staircase arrangement.

The response Site information and marked-up plans were reconciled into a clear drawing structure suitable for Land Registry and lease coordination.

  • Ground and basement plans
  • Demise and access clarity
  • Fire lobby and escape coordination
02

Residential · Wolverhampton

Building Regulations response package

The brief A residential project needed revised plans, structural calculations and a coordinated response to Building Control comments.

The response The architectural and structural information was brought together so the compliance points could be answered as one coherent package.

  • Revised technical drawings
  • Structural calculations
  • Building Control coordination
03

Mixed-use · Birmingham

Multi-level conversion detailing

The brief A complex existing building required detailed plans and sections for a multi-level conversion, with fire, acoustic, ventilation and structural requirements considered together.

The response A coordinated technical set connected layout decisions with the performance requirements of walls, floors, openings and extract systems.

  • Plans and loft sections
  • Fire and acoustic coordination
  • Mechanical extract requirements
04

Residential · West London

Extension plans and structure

The brief Existing property information and site photographs needed to be developed into detailed proposals with structural calculations.

The response The design was grounded in the existing house geometry, with proposed openings and structure coordinated as part of the drawing package.

  • Existing and proposed plans
  • Steel beam coordination
  • Construction-ready technical detail
05

Structural · Walsall

Steel, padstone and foundation package

The brief A property alteration required a full structural package, including beam design, bearing information and a foundation note responsive to site conditions.

The response Calculations and drawing information were structured so the load path, support conditions and construction requirements could be understood together.

  • Beam and padstone calculations
  • Foundation strategy
  • Building Control response notes
06

Heritage · Stoke-on-Trent

Context-led drawing coordination

The brief A town-centre heritage property needed coordinated drawings, details and schedules that respected the established roofscape and building character.

The response Existing context, proposed interventions and detailed elements were organised into a consistent heritage-aware information set.

  • Heritage drawings
  • Roof and context coordination
  • Detail schedules
07

Industrial · Lichfield area

Large-format layout production

The brief An industrial premises layout needed to be coordinated at scale and issued in both presentation and editable technical formats.

The response Drawing information was rationalised for A0 output, with consistent scaling and PDF/DWG deliverables for the wider project team.

  • Industrial layout coordination
  • A0 drawing production
  • PDF and DWG issue formats
08

Planning · Green Belt setting

Site-responsive planning strategy

The brief A rural extension proposal needed a robust understanding of levels, trees, retaining solutions and the relationship between new work and the surrounding landscape.

The response The design review identified the surveys, sections and landscape information needed to present a clear, context-led planning case.

  • Topographical and tree information
  • Before-and-after sections
  • Landscape and retaining strategy
